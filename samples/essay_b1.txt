Last summer I have visited London with my family. It was the most interesting trip in my life. We was walking a lot and I learned many new informations about British culture.

On the first day we went to the British Museum. There is a lot of ancient things there, for example the Rosetta Stone. My brother was boring because he don't like museums, but I could stay there all day.

The next day we visited the Tower of London and saw the Crown Jewels. In the evening we eat fish and chips near the river. The weather was not so bad as people say, it rained only two times.

In conclusion, I think London is a great city for tourists. I would like to come back there again and to improve my English with native speakers.
